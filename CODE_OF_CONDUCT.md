# Code of Conduct

OncallKit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the people who use and build this project. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in project spaces or in public spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting on the repository, or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow the code of conduct may be removed from the project.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
